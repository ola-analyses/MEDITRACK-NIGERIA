# MEDITRACK NIGERIA - Business Analysis Project

# Introduction
MediTrack Nigeria is a proposed digital patient management system for Nigerian public hospitals. It provides USSD-based appointment booking (*456*MEDI#) that works on any mobile phone, real-time queue management across all departments, digital patient records accessible by any authorised clinician, digital payment receipting with automatic SMS confirmation, and a live operations dashboard for the Medical Director and Ministry officials. The project focuses on developing a business case, documenting business requirements, and mapping current and proposed hospital processes to identify opportunities for improvement

# Problem Statement
Public hospitals in Nigeria face operational challenges that can affect patient experience and the quality of service delivery. In some hospital settings, patients arrive early in the morning and spend several hours waiting to see a doctor. Reliance on paper-based records, manual processes, and verbal communication can make it difficult to manage patient flow, coordinate hospital departments, and monitor daily operations effectively.
The key problems identified are:
 - No appointment Booking system
- Paper records are lost constantly
-  No inter-departmental visibility
 - Revenue leakage from cash payments
- Management has no real-time data

  # Project Objective
  The main objective of MediTrack Nigeria is to improve patient management and operational efficiency in Nigerian public hospitals through a proposed digital patient management system.
  The project aims to help hospital management identify opportunities to improve:
- Patient waiting times and queue management
- Appointment booking through USSD and web channels
- Patient record accessibility and retrieval
- Coordination and referrals across hospital departments
- Payment tracking and financial accountability
- Patient flow and departmental workload management
- Real-time operational monitoring and reporting
- Accessibility for patients using basic mobile phones
- System reliability during internet connectivity and power interruptions

 # PROCESS OVERVIEW

 
 **Current State (AS-IS)**

 The current-state process illustrates a typical manual patient journey, highlighting opportunities to improve registration, record retrieval, departmental coordination, and payment processing.
 
Patients → Hospital Arrival → Registration/Appointment Queue →Manual File Retrieval → Doctor Consultation  →Manual Lab/Pharmacy Billing →Manual Cashier Payment →Patients

[View the Current-State Process Map](https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVHiil0-c=/)

**Future State (TO-BE)**

The proposed future-state process illustrates how MediTrack Nigeria could support appointment booking, patient check-in, queue management, digital records, referrals, and payment tracking.

USSD/Web Appointment Booking → SMS Confirmation or Reminder → Hospital Check-in → Electronic Queue Management → Doctor Consultation and Digital Record Access → Departmental Referral → Digital Billing and Payment Recording → SMS Receipt Confirmation → Completion of Visit
